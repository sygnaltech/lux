We have FREE parking!

Lux offers free parking for one car per client, for the duration of your appointment, to make your visit painless.

IMPORTANT - Please scan the QR code at reception within 15 minutes of arriving in the car park and inform our reception team if you have used one of our parking spaces.
Please note that Lux Radiology will not be liable for any parking tickets or breach notices issued if you fail to register your vehicle or scan the QR code within the given timeframe.

‍Our parking is available at the Wilson Carpark on 536 Karangahape Rd.

  • The car park entrance is via Gundry St.
  • Turn right as you enter the car park
  • We have 8 Carpark bays marked "Ponsonby Doctors & Lux Radiology" along the mural wall.

You may park in here for the duration of your appointment. Kindly vacate the park at the conclusion of your appointment to allow other patients to access these.

Important parking notes:

Lux Radiology provides free car parking for ONE vehicle per patient.

Please understand that our carparks are shared with the medical center, and we can accommodate one car per patient in fairness to others. If your family members would like to accompany you for your scan and are arriving in separate vehicles, do let them know that there are many carparks operated by Wilson within the same site, as well as street parking around the clinic. Thank you for your understanding.

These parking bays are complimentary for your use for the duration of your appointment. Do note that any significant overstays before or beyond the appointment time may result in ticketing by Wilson Carpark agents. If our Wilson Carpark bays are full, please call reception for alternative parking.

To get to the clinic:

From the Wilson car park, cross Gundry St and walk down the pedestrian footpath along K road until you reach the Ponsonby Doctors clinic ( look out for the bright LED screen ). We are located within this clinic.

There are currently construction works going on in the building next to us so please keep to the designated footpath.
