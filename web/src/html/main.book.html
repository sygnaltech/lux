Prepare & Attend

What to bring to your scan

We'll complete payment before your appointment, so please arrive 5-10 minutes before your scheduled booking.
  1. Your referral form. Please bring your printed referral form with you.
  2. Any prior scan images & reports. If you have previously had medical imaging performed on the same area of your body, please bring those previous images and reports so that our radiographer can provide the best comparative scans.
  3. Any growth chart. If you have a growth chart, please bring this.

Preparing for your scan

When scanning these areas, correct preparation is essential.

Other examination areas

For all other areas, no preparation required.

Gallbladder or upper abdomen

Please prepare as follows;

  • Nothing to eat 6 hours prior to your scan and only water to drink. If you are diabetic, please consult with your Doctor.
  • Continue taking your medication as normal.
Dating pregnancy scans

Please prepare as follows;

  • Please arrive to your scan with a full bladder. Empty your bladder one hour prior then drink 3-4 glasses of water,
  • If your bladder is not full ten minutes prior to your examination, please drink more water. If you are uncomfortably full you may let some out.

Other pregnancy scan types do not require preparation.

Renal, kidney, bladder, prostate, lower abdomen, female pelvis, gynae

Please prepare as follows;

  • Please arrive to your scan with a full bladder. Empty your bladder one hour prior then drink 3-4 glasses of water,
  • If your bladder is not full ten minutes prior to your examination, please drink more water. If you are uncomfortably full you may let some out.
Abdomen or pelvis

Please prepare as follows;

  • Nothing to eat 6 hours prior to your scan and only water to drink. If you are diabetic, please consult with your Doctor.
  • Continue taking your medication as normal.
  • Please arrive to your scan with a full bladder. Empty your bladder one hour prior then drink 3-4 glasses of water,
  • If your bladder is not full ten minutes prior to your examination, please drink more water. If you are uncomfortably full you may let some out.

For lower abdomen scans specifically, see the above item for preparation instructions.

Our cancellation policy

‍We hope you appreciate that any time is valuable, not only to us but to other patients wanting to book in.

  • No cancellations or changes allowed within 4 hours of the appointment.
  • If you cannot make your appointment, kindly notify us 24 hours before your appointment, to allow your slot to be utilised by another patient.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours will incur a 50% cancellation fee.
  • Non-attendance will incur a 100% cancellation fee ( the full cost of the appt. ).
  • If a cancellation fee is due, an invoice will be emailed to you. If the invoice is not settled by the end of the calendar month, Lux Radiology reserves the right to forward the unpaid invoice to a debt collection agency. Additional charges may apply for the debt collection process. Proof of payment must be emailed back to us.
