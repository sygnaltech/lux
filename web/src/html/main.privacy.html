Privacy Policy

Our privacy policy in respect of any information that we may gather as a result of you using our website is as follows:

1. Anonymous Log information

When you visit our website our servers automatically record information that your browser sends whenever you visit a website. These server logs may include information such as your web request, Internet Protocol address, browser type, browser language, the date and time of your request and one or more cookies that may uniquely identify your browser. This information is non-personally identifying information and we collect this information so as to understand how browsers use our website and to administer the site.

2. Cookies

When you visit our site we send one or more cookies, a small file containing a string of characters, to your computer or other device that uniquely identifies your browser. We use cookies to improve the quality of our service, including for storing user preferences and tracking user trends. If you do not wish to have cookies placed on your computer you should set your browser to refuse cookies before using our website, however certain features of our website may not function properly without cookies.

3. User Communications

When you visit a public area of our website you remain anonymous. However, when you send an email or other communication to us, or otherwise provide us with personally identifiable information, then you will not remain anonymous. We may retain those communications in order to process your enquiries, respond to your requests and improve our services.

4. Links

We may present links in a format that enables us to keep track of whether these links have been followed. We use this information to improve the quality and organization of our content.

5. Privacy Policy Changes

We may change our privacy policy from time to time and at our discretion. All changes will be posted on our website and may apply to previously collected information. Nothing in this policy prevents us from passing on information gathered on this site to any successor organisations.
